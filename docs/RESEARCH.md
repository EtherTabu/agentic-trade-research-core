# Research method and unanswered questions

The calculator accepts an externally supplied hypothesis encoded as boolean signals. It does not discover, rank or originate strategies. A completed signal bar is followed by next-bar-open entry, a fixed holding horizon and explicit per-side entry-notional costs. Output includes a potentially overlapping unconditional baseline; neither selected observations nor the baseline are assumed statistically independent.

A credible independent evaluation would freeze the hypothesis and dataset rules before examining holdout outcomes, use untouched temporal/regime samples, account for multiple comparisons, validate calendar/missing-session/source timing, and model actual liquidity, execution costs and survivorship. Those conditions are not automatically enforced by these calculations.

Journal statistics describe closed records, separating paper, live and passed kinds. Net P&L must already include all costs. Simultaneous closes are grouped to avoid row-order artifacts. This is not a broker-reconciled, cash-flow-adjusted account return series. Synthetic sample gains/losses have no financial evidentiary value.

Remaining questions: Are signals prospectively available? Does a result survive an independent holdout, changing regimes and realistic costs? Are sample sizes adequate? Can uncertainty and missed/no-fill outcomes be represented without hindsight? Are dataset rights compatible with publication? No answer is invented by a passing unit test.

The private project preserves losing paper outcomes and unresolved evidence; no repeatable positive expectancy has been established. Nothing here authorizes strategy promotion, risk changes, paid inference or live trading.
